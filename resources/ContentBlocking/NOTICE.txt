Lite native content blocking

Filter data derived from uBlock Origin Lite by Raymond Hill and contributors:
https://github.com/uBlockOrigin/uBOL-home
Version and exact source revision: provenance.json

uBlock Origin Lite is licensed under GNU GPL version 3 or, at your option,
any later version. See COPYING-uBOL.txt. It is provided without warranty.
The unmodified input files and upstream license are in upstream-source.tar.xz.
The conversion source is script/import_ubol.py in the Lite source tree.
Lite's native adapter is lite/browser/LTContentBlocker.h/.mm and LTEngine.mm.
This integration is a subset and is not an official uBlock Origin Lite release.

Included rulesets: uBlock filters, EasyList, EasyPrivacy, Peter Lowe's list,
uBlock Badware, and Online Malicious URL Blocklist (URLhaus).
Upstream ruleset names and home URLs are retained in the source archive's
chromium/rulesets/ruleset-details.json. No upstream JavaScript is executed.
